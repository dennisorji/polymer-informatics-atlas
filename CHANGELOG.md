# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — 2026-09-14

Initial research release of the Polymer Informatics Atlas.

Included:

- six executed notebooks covering data audit, representation design, property-specific modelling, chemistry-aware generalization, shared learning, predictive reliability, and final interpretation;
- release-facing result tables and compact summary figures;
- exact Morgan-regeneration and environment-count integrity checks;
- chemistry-separated stress tests distinct from random-holdout evaluation;
- masked multitask and complete-case ablation analyses;
- fixed split-conformal reliability analysis for Tc, Density, and Rg;
- final structure–property synthesis, property archetypes, and an illustrative Tc–Density Pareto design scenario;
- reproducibility, data-provenance, license, and citation documentation.

The raw competition data and large regenerated model artifacts are intentionally excluded from version control.
